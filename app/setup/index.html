Can’t find or open your frame?

Keep it powered on and nearby. An unconnected frame opens setup for five minutes after startup; restart it to open another window. If your frame is already connected to WiFi, open its app instead of setting it up again.

After setup, your phone and frame must be on the same WiFi. Guest networks may block devices from reaching each other.

On Android, if the frame’s page won’t open, go to Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Nearby devices and choose Allow, then retry the link. If Allow already appears selected, tap it again.
